# Watch someone use it: a ten-minute check

For acceptance criterion 11 of spec 004. Do it with one older relative and one teenager; more is better. It costs nothing and finds what no automated test can.

## Rules for you

- Hand over the phone with the app freshly installed (so the tutorial shows). Say only: "This is an app for our Umrah trip. I'd like to see how it feels. There are no wrong answers."
- **Do not help, point or explain.** If they ask, say "what would you try?". Silence is the test.
- Note where they pause, what they tap that does nothing, and any word they read aloud with a question in their voice.

## Three tasks

| # | Say this | Passes if | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Open it and tell me what is happening on the trip today." | They get through or skip the tutorial and read out the green card on Today, within about a minute | |
| 2 | "What is the first thing tomorrow?" | They find it on Today or on Plan | |
| 3 | "Your son has sent you this code: K7MQ-2XWP. Join his group." (Any code; it will say the code isn't valid, which is fine.) | They reach the screen where a code is typed. Note whether "you need an account" stops them | |

## Afterwards, ask

1. Was any writing too small?
2. Was there a button you were unsure about pressing?
3. What do you think the four words at the bottom do?

## What to bring back

The notes, as they are. Each hesitation becomes either a small fix or a line in a later spec.
